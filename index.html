Yuki Ip  ·  AI character animation

One character. Four pieces.

JJ Befuddlez is an AI character who sings in lip-synced music videos and talks to camera in vlogs. The music videos are musical comedy: an everyday scene, a song, and a joke. The vlogs are observational and a little funny. Each piece tests the same question: does the character stay itself from one shot to the next?

◆ On these pieces

JJ is my own character, built to test one AI character across formats: sung and spoken, vertical and wide. Getting one usable shot takes many tries, so most of the work is choosing which shots to keep and cutting them to the sound.

01 Musical comedy · Lip sync · Vertical

JJ Befuddlez — Treadmill

Character · Direction · AI generation · Edit

A 19-second musical comedy. An everyday scene, a trip to the gym, turns into a song with a joke in it. The character sings to camera, with cutaways to the treadmill, torn-paper collage backgrounds, and lyric words on screen as they are sung.

◆ Relevant to a song-led film

An everyday moment, a song, and a joke. The gym gag arrives on the line that sets it up, then the edit comes back to the character's face. The picture is built around the lyric, not laid over the song afterward.

02 Musical comedy · Lip sync · Vertical

JJ Befuddlez — Router

Character · Direction · AI generation · Edit

A 36-second musical comedy built from home life. The character dances through a living room while a robot vacuum and a glowing router get their own moments in the song. The light moves from sparkly blue to a darker, moodier red as the song builds.

◆ Relevant to a song-led film

Everyday objects are the jokes, and the character is the warmth. The vacuum appears on the line about vacuums, and the router closes the song with a burst of light. The set changes around the character, so the song has a shape from start to finish.

03 Observational vlog · Wide

JJ Befuddlez — Street vlog

Character · Script · Direction · AI generation · Edit

A 77-second observational vlog on a social issue, told with a light, funny touch. The character talks to camera outside a coffee shop and asks where the beans come from. Hand-lettered words land on screen as it speaks, and the edit moves between wide shots and tight close-ups.

◆ Relevant to a song-led film

The close-ups are the hard test. Eyes, fur, and expression have to read as the same character shot after shot, including side glances and head turns. This is the piece where you can judge that for yourself.

04 Observational vlog · Wide

JJ Befuddlez — Roof vlog

Character · Script · Direction · AI generation · Edit

A 65-second observational vlog on a social issue, also with a light, funny touch. The character sits on a rooftop at sunset and talks to camera. A cardboard sign, a city skyline, and a closing question for the viewer finish the piece.

◆ Relevant to a song-led film

A new place, the same character. The street gave way to a rooftop and the light changed to golden hour, but the character is easy to recognize. A format like this can carry more than one story.

The through-line

Four pieces, one character. The work is choosing which shots to keep, cutting them to the sound, and keeping the character recognizable from one format to the next.
